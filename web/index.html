How to play

  • Press Start to begin a run.
  • Press A to jump over the boxes. Press it again in the air to double jump.
  • Every column of boxes you clear scores a point. Touch one and it’s game over.
  • Start pauses. Your best score is saved on the cartridge (here, in this browser).

About

PandaJump began in 2014 as a Flappy Bird–style browser game. This is its remake for the original Game Boy: a real ROM written in C with GBDK-2020, running here in an emulator. Download it to play in any Game Boy emulator, or on the real thing with a flash cartridge.
